## A. The rules, applied the same way to everyone

**Must-haves**
- **M1 Degree.** Marketing, business administration or a related field. Commerce, management, economics, communications, mass communication, media and public relations all count as related. A postgraduate qualification in the field (MBA, master's, postgraduate diploma) counts as met, because it presupposes a first degree. A degree with no subject on the CV is "confirm at screening", never "not met". Only a clearly unrelated subject with nothing in the field above it is "not met".
- **M2 Ten or more years of marketing.** Counted from dated marketing roles. Sales or accounting years don't count.
- **M3 Five or more years in marketing leadership.** Manager, head, director, VP or founder roles.
- **M4 Two to three years in senior leadership.** Head, director, VP, chief, general manager or founder.
- **M5 Relocation.** Already in the UAE means met. Anywhere else, it's a question for screening, not a failure.

**Nice-to-haves**
- **N1** Retail, malls, entertainment, hospitality, consumer brands or mixed-use.
- **N2** GCC or UAE marketing.
- **N3** Arabic.
- **N4** MBA or a professional marketing qualification.
- **N5** Work in Korea, Hong Kong, Vietnam, China, Japan or Singapore.
