Source of truth for every commercial surface. Any deviation on

## 1. Category definition (say this everywhere)

**TaaSFlow is an on-demand recruiting function delivered through a
transparent product — recruiting execution + a live workspace +
AI-supported evaluation, on a flat subscription.**

We are not:
- an ATS
- a job board
- a marketplace of freelance recruiters
- an "AI recruiter" replacing humans
- a staffing agency charging % of salary

We are: **your hiring team, on demand.**

## 4. The six pillars (order matters — always in this order)

| # | Pillar | Short form |
|---|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Your hiring team, on demand | Recruiters when you need them, off when you don't |
| 2 | Ranked candidates, not CV dumps | Every candidate scored against your requirements with evidence |
| 3 | Human recruiters + AI-supported structure | People do the judgement; AI enforces the structure |
| 4 | Live workspace visibility | Every candidate, score, and stage is watchable in real time |
| 5 | Subscription, not placement fees | Flat monthly fee — never a share of the salary |
| 6 | You own pipeline and decisions | We recommend; you decide. Candidates stay yours. |

Every marketing page must surface **at least three** of the six,
and never in a different order.

## 6. Value-prop hierarchy (approved order — everywhere)

1. **What you get:** ranked candidates with evidence (outcome)
2. **How it's produced:** human recruiters + AI-supported structure (method)
3. **Where you see it:** the live workspace (surface)
4. **How you pay:** flat subscription, no placement fees (model)
5. **What you own:** the pipeline and every decision (control)
6. **Why now:** hiring should be explainable, not opaque (belief)

Never invert. Never lead with pricing. Never lead with AI.

## 7. Banned phrases (do not ship)

| ❌ Banned | Why |
|----------|-----|
| "AI-powered recruiting platform" | Reduces us to software; hides the human recruiters |
| "AI recruiter" / "AI does the hiring" | We are AI-supported, not AI-driven |
| "Recruiting SaaS" | We're not SaaS. We're a service delivered through product |
| "Applicant tracking system" / "ATS" (for us) | We're not an ATS |
| "Talent marketplace" / "hiring marketplace" | Marketplace implies matching, not execution |
| "CV dump" / "resume pile" | Only allowed in contrast: "ranked candidates, not CV dumps" |
| "Placement fee" / "% of salary" (for us) | Only allowed to describe what we do **not** charge |
| "World-class" / "best-in-class" / "cutting-edge" / "revolutionary" | Generic, unearned |
| "Growing companies" / "modern teams" | Replace with specifics: Series A–C, 50–5,000 employees |
| "Talent as a Service platform" | We're a function, not a platform |
| "End-to-end hiring solution" | Vague. Say the six pillars instead |
| "Human-in-the-loop AI" | Jargon. Say "human recruiters + AI-supported structure" |
| "Taasflow" / "TAAS" / "TaaS Flow" / "taasflow" | Product name is **TaaSFlow** — one word, caps as shown |
| "Book a demo" (standalone) | Demo of what? Use "Book a scoped call" / "Start a role" |
