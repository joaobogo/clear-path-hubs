# Client Admin Guide

You are the primary account holder for your organization. You can invite teammates, create positions, and manage every candidate.

## Common actions

1. **Create a position** — click **New position** on your dashboard; the intake wizard guides you.
   ![TODO screenshot — new position]()
2. **Invite a teammate** — `/settings/team` → **Invite** → choose role (Editor/Viewer).
   ![TODO screenshot — invite]()
3. **Review candidates** — open a position; you see delivered candidates on the Kanban board.
   ![TODO screenshot — Kanban]()
4. **Move a candidate** — drag between columns or use the menu; every move is logged.
5. **Schedule an interview** — open the candidate → **Schedule**.
   ![TODO screenshot — interview]()
6. **Message the recruiter** — thread on the candidate drawer.

## Roles at a glance

- **Admin** (you): everything.
- **Editor**: view + move candidates + schedule interviews.
- **Viewer**: view only.
