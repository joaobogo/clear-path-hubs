# Client Editor Guide

You review candidates and coordinate interviews.

## What you can do

- Review delivered candidates.
- Move candidates through the Kanban board.
- Schedule interviews.
- Message the recruiter.

## What you can't do

- Invite teammates or change roles → ask your admin.
- Create or edit positions → ask your admin.
