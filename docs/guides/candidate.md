# Candidate Guide

You applied to a role via TaaSFlow.

## Track your application

1. Sign in at `/auth` with the email you applied with.
2. Your applications are at `/me/applications`.
   ![TODO screenshot — my applications]()
3. Each application shows a plain-language status: *Application received · Under review · Shared with employer · Shortlisted · Interview scheduled · Offer · Congratulations · Not moving forward*.

## Messages

If the recruiter reaches out, you'll see a message + email. Reply from the application page.
![TODO screenshot — messages]()

## Your data

- **See a copy** of what we hold on you: `/me/privacy` → **Download**.
- **Correct** something: `/me/privacy` → **Request correction**.
- **Delete** your data: `/me/privacy` → **Delete account**.

We reply within 30 days.
